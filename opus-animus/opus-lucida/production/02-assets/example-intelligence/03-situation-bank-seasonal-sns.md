# Situation Bank - Seasonal / SNS
**Status:** Active draft  
**Role:** Current-feeling situations for hooks, shorts, and selective long-form seasoning

---

## 1. Use Rule

Seasonal and SNS-linked situations should add freshness, not replace clarity.

Recommended ratio:

```text
Long-form:
80% evergreen
20% seasonal / SNS

Shorts:
50-70% evergreen
30-50% seasonal / SNS
```

Use these when Lucida needs:

- a stronger first 5 seconds;
- a more current short-form angle;
- a bridge from grammar to daily digital behavior;
- a modern situation without becoming meme-dependent.

## 4. Best Use Cases

### Best for shorts

1. `SNS-01 Group chat seen but no reply`
2. `SNS-02 Concert / event conflict`
3. `SNS-04 Meetup misunderstanding`

### Best as a light long-form seasoning

1. `SNS-02` after an evergreen main story
2. `SNS-04` when teaching strong denial or misunderstanding

---

## 5. Guardrails

Do not use seasonal or SNS situations as the main long-form story if:

- the grammar fit is weaker than the evergreen alternative;
- the joke / reference needs outside knowledge;
- the situation will likely feel old in 3-6 months;
- the example becomes more about fandom or gossip than grammar logic.

Preferred approach:

```text
Main lesson example = evergreen
Secondary comparison or short-form angle = seasonal / SNS
```
