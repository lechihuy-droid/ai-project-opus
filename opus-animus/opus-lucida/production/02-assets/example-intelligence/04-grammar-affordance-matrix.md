## Scoring

```text
0 = not useful
3 = usable
5 = very strong fit
```

---

## Wake Cluster Requirements

A strong Wake situation should support at least two of:

```text
correction / misunderstanding
constraint / responsibility
logical conclusion
strong denial
```

Best situations support three or four.

---

## Retrieval Mapping

Recommended label mapping for Wake:

```text
correction
-> わけではない

constraint_logic
-> わけにはいかない

logical_conclusion
-> わけだ

strong_denial_logic
-> わけがない
```

When a situation supports:

```text
correction + constraint_logic
```

it is usually strong for:

```text
hook
hook_quiz
story_spine
worked_example
```

When a situation supports:

```text
strong_denial_logic
```

it is usually stronger for:

```text
comparison
diagnostic_practice
shorts
```
