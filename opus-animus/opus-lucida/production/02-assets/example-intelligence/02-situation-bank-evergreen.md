# Situation Bank - Evergreen
**Status:** Active draft  
**Role:** Stable, reusable situations for long-form lessons, worksheet items, and baseline example generation

---

## 1. Use Rule

Evergreen situations should be the default source for long-form teaching examples.

They are preferred when Lucida needs:

- clear grammar logic;
- high learner relatability;
- low cringe risk;
- examples that still work after six months;
- examples that can expand into worksheet and practice.

## 4. Priority Notes

### Highest-value long-form situations

1. `EG-01 Night before JLPT`
2. `EG-02 Client deadline`
3. `EG-05 Team misunderstanding`

These three cover the strongest balance of:

- exam relevance;
- real-life tension;
- easy contrast between misunderstanding and reality;
- clean expansion into multiple grammar lines.

## 5. Example Seeds

### EG-01 Night Before JLPT

```text
Natural Vietnamese:
Khong phai la minh khong muon di dau.
Nhung mai thi N2 roi, toi nay minh khong di choi duoc that.

Use:
- opening hook
- hook quiz
- retrieval after explanation
```

### EG-02 Client Deadline

```text
Natural Vietnamese:
Khong phai la em khong muon di dau a.
Nhung toi nay em phai gui tai lieu cho khach, nen that su khong di duoc.

Use:
- practical expansion
- real-life promise
- worksheet transfer
```

### EG-05 Team Misunderstanding

```text
Natural Vietnamese:
Khong phai cau ay ghet moi nguoi dau.
Chi la hom nay cau ay ket viec qua thoi.

Use:
- strong denial contrast
- social misunderstanding
- short-form angle
```

---

## 6. Selection Rule

For a Wake-style cluster, prefer situations that naturally support at least two of these:

- correction of misunderstanding;
- real constraint;
- conclusion after hearing the reason;
- strong denial of a false interpretation.

If the situation only produces one line cleanly, it is better as a support example than as the main lesson example.
