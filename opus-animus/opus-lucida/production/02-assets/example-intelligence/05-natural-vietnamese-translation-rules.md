# Natural Vietnamese Translation Rules
**Status:** Active draft
**Role:** Make examples sound natural for Vietnamese learners

---

## Rule

Use two translation layers:

```text
Literal support translation
Natural Vietnamese line
```

Public video should usually speak the natural Vietnamese line.

Use literal support only when explaining form or contrast.

Add one more rule:

```text
Natural Vietnamese line must sound like speech.
Literal support line may sound more technical.
Do not swap their jobs.
```

---

## Examples

```text
行きたくないわけではありません。
Literal: Không phải là tôi không muốn đi.
Natural: Không phải là mình không muốn đi đâu.
```

```text
今日は行くわけにはいきません。
Literal: Hôm nay tôi không thể đi vì có ràng buộc.
Natural: Nhưng hôm nay mình kẹt thật, không đi được.
```

---

## Avoid

```text
stiff textbook Vietnamese
over-literal grammarese
awkward "không thể vì ràng buộc" in public narration unless explaining grammar
```

---

## Spoken-Line Test

Before approving a Vietnamese line, ask:

- would a real teacher say this out loud?
- would a learner say this in the described situation?
- is this shorter and more natural than the literal support line?

If not, rewrite.

---

## Japanese Pairing Rule

Natural Vietnamese should be paired with natural Japanese.

Do not allow this failure mode:

```text
Japanese line sounds textbook
Vietnamese line sounds spoken
```

or the reverse.

Both layers should feel like they belong to the same scene and register.
