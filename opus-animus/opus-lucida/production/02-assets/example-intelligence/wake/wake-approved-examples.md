## 2. Primary Spine

### Situation

Friend invites learner out, but tomorrow is JLPT N2 or a mock test.

### Labels

```text
hook
hook_quiz
worked_example
vn_n3_n2
responsibility
constraint
correction
constraint_logic
logical_conclusion
exam_transfer_high
real_life_transfer_medium
evergreen
low_cringe
needs_social_tension
```

### Why it is approved

- immediate relevance for the core persona;
- easy to understand in a few seconds;
- naturally supports misunderstanding plus constraint;
- strong bridge to quiz, worksheet, and CTA.

### Public-facing Vietnamese

```text
Khong phai minh khong muon di dau.
Nhung mai thi N2 roi, toi nay minh khong di choi duoc dau.
```

### Support translation

```text
Dau phai la toi khong muon di.
Nhung vi ngay mai thi N2 nen toi nay toi khong the di choi.
```

### Japanese natural line

```text
行きたくないわけじゃないんだけど、明日N2だから、今夜は遊びに行くわけにはいかないんだ。
```

### Best use

- opening situation
- hook quiz
- retrieval later in the video

### Best Wake logic fit

- `わけではない`: not refusing the idea itself
- `わけにはいかない`: real constraint because of exam responsibility
- `わけだ`: natural conclusion after hearing the reason

### Watchout

Needs a little social tension so it does not sound like a flat exam excuse.

### Why it is approved

- supports the real-life usage promise;

- creates a clean contrast between desire and obligation;
- easy to reuse in worksheet and shorts.

### Public-facing Vietnamese

```text

```

### Support translation

```text
Khong phai la em khong muon di.
Nhung vi toi nay em phai gui tai lieu cho khach nen em khong the di duoc.
```

### Japanese natural line

```text
行きたくないわけではないんですが、今夜中に資料を送らないといけないので、飲みに行くわけにはいきません。
```

### Best use

- main story expansion
- real-life contrast
- worksheet
- short-form variant

### Best Wake logic fit

- `わけではない`
- `わけにはいかない`
- `わけだ`
- optional third-person contrast if needed

### Watchout

If written too dry, it falls back into textbook office scene territory.
