**Status:** Reviewed draft  
**Role:** Candidate situation pool for the Wake cluster before final approval

---

## 1. Selection Logic

Wake examples are strongest when one situation can support multiple lines such as:

- `わけではない`: correction / not what you think
- `わけにはいかない`: real-life constraint
- `わけだ`: natural conclusion after hearing the reason
- `わけがない`: strong denial of a false reading

If a situation only supports one of these cleanly, keep it as a support example, not the main lesson example.

## 3. Candidate Notes

### WK-01 Night Before JLPT

Public-facing Vietnamese:

```text
Khong phai minh khong muon di dau.
Nhung mai thi N2 roi, toi nay minh khong di choi duoc dau.
```

Support translation:

```text
Dau phai la toi khong muon di.
Nhung vi ngay mai thi N2 nen toi nay toi khong the di choi.
```

Japanese natural line:

```text
行きたくないわけじゃないんだけど、明日N2だから、今夜は遊びに行くわけにはいかないんだ。
```

Use when the lesson needs:

- the strongest possible learner hook;
- early exam relevance;
- a fast hook quiz situation.

### WK-02 Client Deadline

Public-facing Vietnamese:

```text
Em cung muon di lam a.
Nhung toi nay em phai gui tai lieu cho khach, nen that su khong di duoc.
```

Support translation:

```text
Khong phai la em khong muon di.
Nhung vi toi nay em phai gui tai lieu cho khach nen em khong the di duoc.
```

Japanese natural line:

```text
行きたくないわけではないんですが、今夜中に資料を送らないといけないので、飲みに行くわけにはいきません。
```

Use when the lesson needs:

- real-life usage promise;
- practical adult context;
- stronger workplace relevance.

### WK-03 Family Promise

Public-facing Vietnamese:

```text
Khong phai la minh khong muon di dau.
Nhung hom nay minh da hua dua me di vien roi, nen khong ra ngoai duoc.
```

Support translation:

```text
Minh khong phai la khong muon di.
Nhung vi hom nay da hua dua me di benh vien nen khong the ra ngoai.
```

Japanese natural line:

```text
行きたくないわけじゃないんだけど、今日は母を病院に連れていく約束をしてるから、出かけるわけにはいかないんだ。
```

Use when the lesson needs:

- clear duty-based constraint;
- softer, more human emotional tone;
- backup contrast to exam-heavy examples.

### WK-04 Team Misunderstanding

Public-facing Vietnamese:

```text
Lam gi co chuyen cau ay ghet moi nguoi.
Chi la hom nay cau ay ket viec thoi.
```

Support translation:

```text
Khong the nao cau ay ghet moi nguoi.
Chi la hom nay cau ay ban viec thoi.
```

Japanese natural line:

```text
あの人がみんなを嫌っているわけがないよ。今日は仕事が立て込んでいるだけだよ。
```

Use when the lesson needs:

- stronger `わけがない`;
- social misunderstanding;
- short-form repurpose angle.
