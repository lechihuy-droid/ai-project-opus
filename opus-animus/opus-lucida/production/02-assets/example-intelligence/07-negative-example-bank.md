## 1. What Goes Here

Use this file for:

- textbook-feeling situations;
- low-emotion examples;
- examples with weak grammar affordance;
- Vietnamese lines that sound translated rather than spoken;
- trend references that age badly.

## 3. Starter Negative Cases

### NEG-01 Flat Office Scene

```text
Weak example:
Nam works at a Japanese company.
The team invites him to nomikai.
He has a deadline.

Why weak:
Too generic, too little tension, sounds like a workbook setup.

What it accidentally teaches:
That any office context is automatically realistic.

Better pattern:
Specific invitation + fear of being misunderstood + visible real constraint.

Avoid in future:
Office scenes with no emotional pressure or social consequence.
```

### NEG-02 Literal Vietnamese That Nobody Says

```text
Weak example:
Khong phai la toi khong muon di.

Why weak:
The grammar may be fine, but the spoken Vietnamese is too stiff if used as the public line.

What it accidentally teaches:
That direct grammar support lines and natural spoken lines are the same thing.

Better pattern:
Khong phai minh khong muon di dau.

Avoid in future:
Using support translations as narration or public-facing slide copy.
```
