# Script Writing Requirements - Wake Cluster
**Status:** Draft
**Use case:** Dua cho ChatGPT de viet script public/sample video dau tien
**Ref:** `../../00-active/wake-cluster/01-master-teaching-skeleton.md`, `../../framework/lesson-method/02-framework-lesson-method.md`, `../../framework/grammar-3-view/03-framework-3-view-grammar.md`, `../../framework/slide-method/04-slide-method-guideline.md`

## 1. Muc tieu cua script

Script nay duoc viet khong phai de polish cuoi cung, ma de:

- kiem tra tinh dung cua noi dung grammar;
- kiem tra `3 cach nhin` co hoat dong tot voi grammar cluster cung surface form hay khong;
- kiem tra nhip video 12-15 phut;
- kiem tra hook public, comparison, practice va CTA co hop ly khong;
- lam co so de sua framework/phuong cham neu can.

Script phai giup team tra loi:

> Neu quay video theo script nay, nguoi hoc N2 co de hieu hon va phan biet duoc `わけだ`, `わけではない`, `わけがない`, `わけにはいかない` khong?

---

## 2. Audience

Primary audience:

- nguoi Viet dang cuoi N3 hoac moi bat dau N2;
- da gap nhieu cau co `わけ` nhung hay nham logic;
- can cach giai thich de hieu, co dau hieu va co so sanh ro.

Secondary audience:

- nguoi Viet dang on JLPT N2 trong Soumatome/Shinkanzen;
- nguoi di lam o Nhat can phan biet nuance de doc mail, nghe hop va noi lich su.

---

## 3. Video promise

Sau video nay, nguoi hoc phai phan biet duoc:

- `わけだ`
- `わけではない`
- `わけがない`
- `わけにはいかない`

theo logic:

- `わけだ` = thao nao / ra la vay / ket luan hop logic
- `わけではない` = khong phai la / khong den muc la / partial negation
- `わけがない` = khong the nao / impossible
- `わけにはいかない` = khong the lam vi co rang buoc / vi trach nhiem / vi hoan canh

Khong duoc de script bi lech promise nay.

---

## 4. Pain point trung tam

Script phai xoay quanh pain point sau:

> Cung la `わけ`, nhung moi mau lai xu ly mot logic khac nhau. Neu hoc bang dich tieng Viet roi rac, nguoi hoc rat de nham giua ket luan, phu dinh mot phan, phu dinh kha nang, va khong the hanh dong vi rang buoc.

Script phai lam ro:

- `わけだ` = ket luan hop logic tu ly do/thong tin truoc do;
- `わけではない` = phu dinh mot cach mem hon, khong phai phu dinh toan bo;
- `わけがない` = phu dinh rat manh, "khong the nao";
- `わけにはいかない` = van muon/co the lam, nhung rang buoc khien khong the lam.

---

## 5. Scope noi dung

### Mau chinh phai day

- `わけだ`
- `わけではない`
- `わけがない`
- `わけにはいかない`

### Mau co the nhac de so sanh neu can

- `はずだ`
- `というわけではない`
- `ないわけにはいかない` chi nhac rat nhanh neu can, khong day thanh section rieng

### Mau khong dua thanh section rieng trong script

- bat ky grammar ngoai cum `わけ` neu khong phuc vu truc tiep cho nham lan trung tam

Khong duoc mo them nhanh noi dung moi chi de "day nhieu hon".

---

## 6. Format dau ra yeu cau

ChatGPT phai tra ra script theo format de review de scan:

```text
## Slide 01 - [Ten slide]
Role:
On-screen:
Script:
Speaker note:
Pause/Emphasis:
```

Hoac it nhat phai co:

- so slide
- muc dich slide
- on-screen text tom tat
- phan script giao vien noi
- nhan [NHAN], [PAUSE], [CHUYEN DOAN] khi can

Khong tra ra mot khoi van ban dai lien tuc khong chia slide.

---

## 7. Structure bat buoc

Script phai di theo thu tu nay:

1. Warm-up pain point
2. Hook quiz
3. Pain point + vi sao de nham
4. Promise cua video
5. Big picture: cung la `わけ` nhung 4 logic khac nhau
6. Giai thich `3 cach nhin`
7. `わけだ`
8. `わけではない`
9. `わけがない`
10. `わけにはいかない`
11. Comparison map `4 wake`
12. So sanh nham lan gan nhat
13. Dau hieu chon mau
14. Practice
15. Recap
16. CTA worksheet

Duoc phep gom lai thanh production version 16-18 slide, nhung khong duoc bo mat mot phan nao trong logic tren.

---

## 8. Yeu cau cho tung phan

### 8.1 Warm-up

Phai:

- mo bang noi dau chung cua nguoi hoc N2;
- ngan;
- de dong cam;
- khoi dong bang trai nghiem "thay quen mat nhung vao de la loan";
- vao thang pain point trong 3-5 giay dau, khong bat dau bang chao hoi dai;
- neu co cau "Chao ban, minh la..." thi dat sau hook/pain point, khong dat o cau dau tien;
- uu tien mo bang cau hoi/tinh huong kieu:
  "Ban da bao gio gap cam giac nay chua? Hoc den N2 roi, nhin chu nao cung quen, nhung khi phai chon dap an thi lai phan van.";
- sau pain point, dua ngay contrast ngan de nguoi xem thay van de:
  "cung la wake, nhung khac mach logic".

Khong nen:

- mo dau bang list grammar truoc khi tao ly do de quan tam;
- mo dau bang loi chao/intro kenh qua 5-10 giay;
- giai thich method qua som truoc khi nguoi hoc thay pain point.

### 8.2 Hook quiz

Phai:

- co 1 cau quiz mo dau co `わけ`;
- dung dang cau de nguoi hoc co the nham it nhat 2 dap an;
- cho nguoi xem dung lai de chon;
- khong reveal dap an qua som.

### 8.3 Pain point explanation

Phai:

- noi ro "cung mot chu khong co nghia la cung mot logic";
- giai thich vi sao hoc bang dich Viet ngan gon de sai;
- dan vao y "phai nhin theo Nghia cot loi / Hinh thuc / Cach dung".

### 8.4 Big picture

Phai:

- cho nguoi hoc 1 tam ban do tong quan;
- tach 4 logic thanh 4 nhom ro rang:
  - ket luan
  - phu dinh mot phan
  - phu dinh kha nang
  - rang buoc khong the lam

### 8.5 3 cach nhin section

Phai:

- giai thich ngan, ro:
  - Nghia cot loi
  - Hinh thuc / cau truc
  - Cach dung / sac thai
- dung nhu "brand method" cua Lucida;
- de tai su dung cho video sau.

### 8.6 Grammar explanation

Moi mau chinh phai co du:

- nhan nghia ngan gon;
- Nghia cot loi;
- Hinh thuc / cau truc;
- Cach dung / sac thai;
- 1 vi du JLPT hoac hoc tap;
- 1 vi du doi song/cong viec neu hop;
- dau hieu chon mau trong de;
- loi de nham nhat.

### 8.7 Comparison

Phai co 2 lop comparison:

- comparison ngang trong 4 mau `わけ`
- comparison voi mau gan de nham nhat neu can

Comparison phai thuc su giai quyet nham lan, khong chi lap lai nghia.

### 8.8 Practice

Phai:

- co it nhat 3 diem tuong tac;
- dung kieu "dung 3 giay va chon thu";
- co answer explanation theo Nghia cot loi / Hinh thuc / Cach dung.

### 8.9 Recap

Phai:

- rat gon;
- de screenshot;
- chot lai 4 logic lon.

### 8.10 CTA

Phai:

- dan ve worksheet;
- noi ro worksheet giup on lai map 4 logic va bai tap tuong tac;
- khong qua salesy;
- phu hop sample video dau tien.

---

## 9. Tone va style

Script phai:

- de hieu;
- co he thong;
- than thien;
- khong qua hoc thuat;
- khong len lop;
- co cam giac "coach giai thich de hieu";
- co nhip noi chuyen tu nhien.

Script khong duoc:

- qua dai dong;
- giai thich lan man;
- dung qua nhieu tu ngu truu tuong;
- bien thanh bai viet hoc thuat.

---

## 10. Constraints

- muc tieu video: 12-15 phut
- production version: 16-18 slide
- uu tien "it chu tren slide, nhieu ro trong loi noi"
- khong duoc them grammar point ngoai scope thanh section rieng
- khong duoc doi CTA chinh khoi worksheet
- khong duoc bien video thanh bai liet ke 4 mau roi roi

---

## 11. Validation criteria

Khi ChatGPT viet xong, script phai de minh review theo 4 tieu chi:

1. Content Accuracy
- grammar dung khong
- vi du tu nhien khong

2. Pedagogy
- 3 cach nhin co ro khong
- map 4 logic co giai quyet pain point khong

3. Video Flow
- co nhip xem duoc khong
- hook co du manh cho public sample khong
- 3-5 giay dau co cham dung pain point khong
- chao hoi/branding co bi dua len qua som khong
- co qua nhieu ly thuyet lien tuc khong

4. Funnel Fit
- CTA worksheet co tu nhien khong
- recap -> CTA co mem khong

---

## 12. Prompt-ready instruction

Ban co the dua cho ChatGPT instruction ngan sau:

```text
Hay viet script sample video YouTube JLPT N2 cho bai:
"わけだ・わけではない・わけがない・わけにはいかない - cung la wake, nhung logic khac nhau the nao?"

Yeu cau:
- Audience: nguoi Viet cuoi N3 / dau N2
- Muc tieu: test tinh dung cua framework va video flow, chua can polished final
- Video 12-15 phut
- Production version 16-18 slide
- Opening rule: 3-5 giay dau vao thang pain point/tinh huong/contrast; khong mo dau bang chao hoi dai. Neu can gioi thieu giao vien/kenh, dat sau hook.
- Bat buoc theo logic:
  warm-up -> hook quiz -> pain point -> promise -> big picture 4 logic -> 3 cach nhin -> わけだ -> わけではない -> わけがない -> わけにはいかない -> comparison map -> dau hieu chon mau -> practice -> recap -> CTA worksheet
- Moi phan grammar phai co Nghia cot loi / Hinh thuc / Cach dung
- Khong them grammar point ngoai scope thanh section rieng
- Tone: de hieu, co he thong, than thien, coach-like
- Output theo tung slide, moi slide co:
  Role, On-screen, Script, Speaker note, Pause/Emphasis
```

---

## 13. Definition of done

Ban script duoc coi la dat yeu cau khi:

- co the doc va review theo slide;
- giu dung scope sample;
- the hien duoc phuong cham day hoc hien tai;
- du tot de minh dung lam ban review content/method/funnel tiep theo.
