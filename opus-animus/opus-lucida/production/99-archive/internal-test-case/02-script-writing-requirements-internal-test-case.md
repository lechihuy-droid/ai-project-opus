## 1. Muc tieu cua script

Script nay duoc viet khong phai de polish cuoi cung, ma de:

- kiem tra tinh dung cua noi dung grammar;
- kiem tra `3 View Grammar Coaching` co hoat dong that khong;
- kiem tra nhip video 12-15 phut;
- kiem tra story, comparison, practice va CTA co hop ly khong;
- lam co so de sua framework/phuong cham neu can.

Script phai giup team tra loi:

> Neu quay video theo script nay, nguoi hoc N2 co de hieu hon va phan biet duoc `kai`, `gai`, `temade` khong?

---

## 2. Audience

Primary audience:

- nguoi Viet dang cuoi N3 hoac moi bat dau N2;
- da hoc nhieu mau N2 nhung van de nham;
- can giai thich de hieu, co clue va co vi du thuc te.

Secondary audience:

- nguoi Viet dang song/lam viec o Nhat;
- can tieng Nhat thuc te hon cho cong viec va doi song.

---

## 3. Video promise

Sau video nay, nguoi hoc phai phan biet duoc:

- `kai` = dang cong
- `gai` = dang lam
- `temade` = lam den muc do do / co danh doi

Khong duoc de script bi lech promise nay.

---

## 4. Pain point trung tam

Script phai xoay quanh pain point sau:

> Nguoi hoc hay nham `kai` va `gai` vi deu de dich la â€œdangâ€, va hay nham `temade` voi `tame ni` vi deu co the dich la â€œdeâ€, nhung nuance va cach dung khac nhau.

Script phai lam ro:

- `kai` nhin ve cong suc co duoc den dap hay khong
- `gai` nhin ve gia tri cua hanh dong/cong viec
- `temade` co sac thai â€œlam den muc do doâ€, co khi hoi qua, co danh doi

---

## 5. Scope noi dung

### Mau chinh phai day

- `kai ga atte`
- `kai mo naku / kai ga nai`
- `gai`
- `temade / made shite`

### Mau so sanh ngoai bai duoc nhac

- `tame ni`
- `you ni`

### Mau khong dua thanh section rieng trong script

- `dake no koto wa aru`

Co the nhac rat nhanh neu that su can, nhung khong duoc mo them nhanh noi dung moi.

---

## 6. Format dau ra yeu cau

ChatGPT phai tra ra script theo format de review de scan:

```text
## Slide 01 - [Ten slide]
Role:
On-screen:
Script:
Speaker note:
Pause/Emphasis:
```

Hoac it nhat phai co:

- so slide
- muc dich slide
- on-screen text tom tat
- phan script giao vien noi
- nhan [NHAN], [PAUSE], [CHUYEN DOAN] khi can

Khong tra ra mot khoi van ban dai lien tuc khong chia slide.

---

## 7. Structure bat buoc

Script phai di theo thu tu nay:

1. Warm-up pain point
2. Hook quiz
3. Pain point + vi sao de nham
4. Promise cua video
5. Story: Nam o cong ty Nhat
6. Giai thich `3 View`
7. `kai ga atte`
8. `kai mo naku`
9. `gai`
10. `temade`
11. Comparison `kai vs gai`
12. Comparison `temade vs tame ni / you ni`
13. JLPT clue map
14. Practice
15. Recap
16. CTA worksheet

Duoc phep gom lai thanh production version 18 slide, nhung khong duoc bo mat mot phan nao trong logic tren.

---

## 8. Yeu cau cho tung phan

### 8.1 Warm-up

Phai:

- mo bang noi dau chung cua nguoi hoc N2;
- ngan;
- de dong cam;
- khong vao ly thuyet ngay.

### 8.2 Hook quiz

Phai:

- co 1 cau quiz mo dau;
- dung cau co effort + ket qua tot;
- cho nguoi xem dung lai de chon;
- khong reveal dap an qua som.

### 8.3 Pain point explanation

Phai:

- noi ro â€œdich giong khong co nghia la dung giongâ€;
- giai thich vi sao hoc bang nghia roi rac de sai.

### 8.4 Story

Phai:

- dung story `Nam lam o cong ty Nhat`;
- story phuc vu bai hoc, khong minh hoa cho vui;
- ket noi duoc voi cong suc, gia tri cong viec va danh doi.

### 8.5 3 View section

Phai:

- giai thich ngan, ro:
  - Meaning
  - Form
  - Usage
- dung nhu â€œbrand methodâ€ cua Lucida;
- de tai su dung cho video sau.

### 8.6 Grammar explanation

Moi mau chinh phai co du:

- nhan nghia ngan gon;
- Meaning View;
- Form View;
- Usage View;
- 1 vi du JLPT hoac hoc tap;
- 1 vi du cong viec/doi song neu hop;
- clue de nhan ra trong de;
- loi de nham nhat.

### 8.7 Comparison

Phai co 2 comparison ro:

- `kai vs gai`
- `temade vs tame ni / you ni`

Comparison phai thuc su giai quyet nham lan, khong chi lap lai nghia.

### 8.8 Practice

Phai:

- co it nhat 2-3 diem tuong tac;
- dung kieu â€œdung 3 giay va chon thuâ€;
- co answer explanation theo Ý nghĩa / Dạng / Cách dùng.

### 8.9 Recap

Phai:

- rat gon;
- de screenshot;
- chot lai 3 y lon.

### 8.10 CTA

Phai:

- dan ve worksheet;
- noi ro gia tri cua worksheet;
- khong qua salesy;
- phu hop sample video dau tien.

## 9. Tone va style

Script phai:

- de hieu;
- co he thong;
- than thien;
- khong qua hoc thuat;
- khong len lop;
- co cam giac â€œcoach giai thich de hieuâ€;
- co nhip noi chuyen tu nhien.

Script khong duoc:

- qua dai dong;
- giai thich lan man;
- dung qua nhieu tu ngu truu tuong;
- bien thanh bai viet hoc thuat.

---

## 10. Constraints

- muc tieu video: 12-15 phut
- production version: 18 slide
- uu tien â€œit chu tren slide, nhieu ro trong loi noiâ€
- khong duoc them grammar point ngoai scope
- khong duoc doi CTA chinh khoi worksheet
- khong duoc doi story trung tam khoi `Nam o cong ty Nhat`

---

## 11. Validation criteria

Khi ChatGPT viet xong, script phai de minh review theo 4 tieu chi:

1. Content Accuracy
- grammar dung khong
- vi du tu nhien khong

2. Pedagogy
- 3 View co ro khong
- comparison co giai quyet pain point khong

3. Video Flow
- co nhip xem duoc khong
- co qua nhieu ly thuyet lien tuc khong

4. Funnel Fit
- CTA worksheet co tu nhien khong
- recap -> CTA co mem khong

---

## 12. Prompt-ready instruction

Ban co the dua cho ChatGPT instruction ngan sau:

```text
Hay viet script sample video YouTube JLPT N2 cho bai:
"kai / gai / temade - dang cong, dang lam hay lam qua muc?"

Yeu cau:
- Audience: nguoi Viet cuoi N3 / dau N2
- Muc tieu: test tinh dung cua framework va video flow, chua can polished final
- Video 12-15 phut
- Production version 18 slide
- Bat buoc theo logic:
  warm-up -> hook quiz -> pain point -> promise -> story Nam o cong ty Nhat -> 3 View -> kai -> kai mo naku -> gai -> temade -> comparison -> clue map -> practice -> recap -> CTA worksheet
- Moi phan grammar phai co Ý nghĩa / Dạng / Cách dùng
- Khong them grammar point ngoai scope
- Tone: de hieu, co he thong, than thien, coach-like
- Output theo tung slide, moi slide co:
  Role, On-screen, Script, Speaker note, Pause/Emphasis
```

## 13. Definition of done

Ban script duoc coi la dat yeu cau khi:

- co the doc va review theo slide;
- giu dung scope sample;
- bieu lo duoc phuong cham day hoc hien tai;
- du tot de minh dung lam ban review content/method/funnel tiep theo.
