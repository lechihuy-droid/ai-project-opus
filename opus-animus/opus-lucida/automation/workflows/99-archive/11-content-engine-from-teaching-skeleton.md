## 0. Muc tieu

Workflow nay chot mot nguyen tac:

> 1 teaching skeleton phai sinh duoc nhieu content, khong chi 1 script video.

Muc tieu la:

- tao nhieu content nhanh hon
- giu thong diep dong bo
- tranh review chong cheo
- dung 1 lan review de phuc vu nhieu asset

---

## 1. Source of truth

Source of truth cho content engine khong phai:

- script rieng le
- slide rieng le
- short rieng le

Ma la:

- `master teaching skeleton`

Script, slide, worksheet, shorts, post, CTA deu la derivative layers.

Ngon ngu uu tien trong giang day tieng Nhat:

- `3 cach nhin` hoac `Nghia - Hinh - Dung`
- `Dau hieu chon mau`
- `Cum tu di truoc`
- `Cum tu di sau`
- `Mach logic cua cau noi`

---

## 2. Content lanes tu 1 skeleton

### Lane A - Teaching lane

- long video
- script
- slide
- worksheet

### Lane B - Reach lane

- shorts
- carousel
- text post
- email seed

### Lane C - Conversion lane

- CTA copy
- lead magnet seed
- landing seed

---

## 3. Expansion logic

Tu skeleton, rut ra 6 loai hat giong:

1. hook core
2. pain point core
3. promise core
4. comparison core
5. clue core
6. CTA core

Moi hat giong phai duoc tai su dung o nhieu asset.

---

## 4. Step-by-step

### Step 1 - Lock skeleton

Chi bat dau content engine khi skeleton da co:

- hook
- pain point
- promise
- 3 View core
- comparison
- clue map
- practice
- CTA

### Step 2 - Create content matrix

Tao:

- message bank
- long-form map
- mid-form map
- short-form map

Output:

- `content matrix`

### Step 3 - Generate teaching assets

Sinh:

- script
- slide deck
- worksheet

### Step 4 - Generate reach assets

Sinh:

- shorts
- carousel
- text post
- email seed

### Step 5 - Generate conversion assets

Sinh:

- CTA lines
- lead magnet framing
- landing seed

### Step 6 - Cross-asset QA

Kiem tra:

- co asset nao lech promise khong
- co asset nao mo ra thong diep moi khong
- co asset nao CTA khac lane chinh khong

---

## 5. Review rule

Khong review moi asset nhu 1 bai hoc moi.

Review theo 3 lop:

### Lop 1 - Skeleton review

Sua logic tai nguon.

### Lop 2 - Lane review

- teaching lane
- reach lane
- conversion lane

### Lop 3 - Bundle review

Kiem tra tat ca co thong nhat khong.

---

## 6. Content abundance principle

Muc tieu khong phai la 1 bai = 1 video.

Muc tieu la:

- 1 skeleton
- 1 video dai
- 1 worksheet
- 1 slide deck
- 3-5 shorts
- 1 carousel
- 1 text post
- 1 email seed
- 3-5 CTA lines

Neu chua ra duoc nhieu asset nhu vay, skeleton chua du "content-rich".
