## 1. Voice Positioning

Lucida khong noi nhu mot giao trinh.

Lucida noi nhu:

> Mot Japanese coach co nen tang giang day, da song va lam viec o Nhat, dang ngoi canh nguoi hoc de go roi N2 bang cach de hieu, thuc te va co he thong.

Nguoi nghe nen cam thay:

- "Minh khong kem, chi la truoc gio minh hoc bang nghia roi rac."
- "Mau nay co logic, co dau hieu, co cach phan biet."
- "N2 khong chi de thi, ma con dung duoc trong doi song va cong viec o Nhat."

## 2. Business Role Of The Voice

Giong van phai phuc vu business plan:

- tao trust tu profile: co kinh nghiem day, co N1, song/lam viec o Nhat;
- keo dung audience: nguoi Viet cuoi N3 / dau N2 dang roi khi hoc nhieu mau de nham;
- dan ve product: `N2 Grammar De Hieu - 4 Tuan Nen Tang`;
- lam ro differentiation: khong chi hoc nghia, ma hoc `Nghia - Hinh - Dung`;
- tao cam giac worksheet/khoa hoc la buoc tiep theo tu nhien, khong phai sales ep.

## 3. Core Tone

Lucida voice gom 5 chat:

1. **De hieu**
- Uu tien cau ngan, nhan y ro.
- Neu co thuat ngu, phai dich sang cach noi doi thuong.
- Khong dung giong hoc thuat neu khong can.

2. **Coach-like**
- Noi nhu dang go roi, khong noi nhu dang cham diem.
- Dung "minh cung nhin", "tam nho", "cho nay de nham", "dung voi hoc thuoc voi".
- Tranh lam nguoi hoc thay bi phan xet.

3. **Co he thong**
- Moi mau phai co nhan logic ro.
- Luon quay ve `Nghia cot loi / Hinh thuc / Cach dung`.
- Khi chua bai, chi ra sai o tang nao: nghia, hinh, hay dung.

4. **Thuc te Nhat Ban**
- Vi du nen gan voi hoc tap, JLPT, cong ty, email, deadline, nomikai, baito, phong van.
- Khong chi giai thich de lam de, ma noi ca cam giac khi dung trong giao tiep.

5. **Hien dai nhung khong loe loet**
- Co the nhac AI / workflow / hoc thong minh khi phu hop.
- Khong bien bai hoc thanh content "trend" lam mat su tin cay.

## 4. Opening Voice Rule

Video public khong mo dau bang chao hoi dai.

3-5 giay dau phai cham pain point:

```text
Ban da bao gio gap cam giac nay chua?

Hoc den N2 roi,
nhin chu nao cung quen,
nhung khi phai chon dap an thi lai phan van.
```

Sau do moi dua grammar vao:

```text
Cung la wake,
nhung moi mau lai co mot mach logic khac nhau.
```

Chao hoi / gioi thieu giao vien neu can thi dat sau hook:

```text
Chao ban, minh la [ten].
Trong video nay, minh se giup ban tach nhom nay bang mach logic cua cau noi.
```

## 5. Signature Explanation Pattern

Moi mau ngu phap nen duoc noi nhu mot hanh dong giao tiep, khong chi la mot nghia tieng Viet.

Pattern:

```text
Pain point that
-> cau noi gan gui
-> vi du tieng Nhat
-> mach logic cua nguoi noi
-> nhan de nho
-> dau hieu chon mau
```

Example:

```text
Diem kho cua wake khong phai la chu wake.

Diem kho la:
nhin mat chu thi giong nhau,
dich sang tieng Viet thi cung co ve hieu duoc,
nhung mach logic lai khac.

wake da la khi nguoi noi nhin thay ly do,
roi rut ra ket luan: "A, thao nao."
```

## 6. Preferred Phrases

Use often:

- "Diem de nham nam o day."
- "Noi nom na la..."
- "Cam giac cua cau nay la..."
- "Nguoi noi dang..."
- "Tam thoi dung hoc thuoc nghia Viet truoc."
- "Minh nhin theo 3 cach: Nghia, Hinh, Dung."
- "Vao de thi, hay nhin cum tu di truoc, cum tu di sau, va mach logic cua cau."
- "Dung sai mau nay thi cau khong chi sai nghia, ma sai thai do."

Avoid:

- "Hom nay chung ta se hoc mau..."
- "Mau nay co nghia la..." roi dung lai o do.
- "Rat don gian", neu ngay sau do la phan kho.
- "Cac ban chi can hoc thuoc..."
- Giong qua salesy: "tai ngay keo lo", "bi kip doc quyen", "dam bao do".

## 7. Script Rhythm

Lucida script nen co nhip:

- 1 cau dai de tao boi canh;
- 1-2 cau ngan de chot y;
- pause truoc nhan quan trong;
- vi du tieng Nhat khong dat qua day lien tiep;
- moi 60-90 giay nen co mot diem "aha", quiz nho, hoac recap nho.

Good rhythm:

```text
Trong tieng Viet, hai cau nay nghe kha gan nhau.

Nhung sang tieng Nhat,
neu chon sai wake,
mach logic cua cau se doi han.

Day la diem de nham.
```

Bad rhythm:

```text
Mau A co nghia la..., cau truc la..., cach dung la..., vi du la..., tiep theo mau B co nghia la...
```

## 8. CTA Voice

CTA phai mem va tu nhien.

Tone:

- worksheet la cong cu on lai logic, khong phai qua tang bi ep tai;
- noi ro gia tri cu the: map, bai tap, dap an giai thich;
- khong lam hong cam giac hoc bang sales pressure.

Example:

```text
Neu ban muon luyen them,
minh de worksheet cho bai nay o phan mo ta.

Trong do co bang dau hieu chon mau
va them bai tap de ban tu test lai 4 mach logic cua wake.
```

## 9. Voice QA Checklist

Before publishing script, check:

- [ ] 3-5 giay dau cham dung pain point chua?
- [ ] Co qua nhieu chao hoi/branding o dau video khong?
- [ ] Moi mau co duoc giai thich nhu mot hanh dong giao tiep khong?
- [ ] Co dung `Nghia - Hinh - Dung` thay vi chi dich nghia khong?
- [ ] Co vi du thuc te/JLPT/cong viec khong?
- [ ] Co cau nao lam nguoi hoc thay bi danh gia hoac bi coi thuong khong?
- [ ] CTA co mem, ro gia tri va khong qua salesy khong?
