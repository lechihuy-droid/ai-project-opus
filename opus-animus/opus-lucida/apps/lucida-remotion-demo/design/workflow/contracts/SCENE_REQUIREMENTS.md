# SceneRequirements Contract

Semantic and representational requirements produced by G05.

## Required
- scene ID and source evidence
- content type and hierarchy
- visual evidence, data, diagram, code, chart, quote, or demonstration needs
- text-density and accessibility constraints
- visual-beat opportunities linked to timing cue IDs
- forbidden representation patterns

## Prohibited
Concrete style, asset, component, or motion preset IDs.
