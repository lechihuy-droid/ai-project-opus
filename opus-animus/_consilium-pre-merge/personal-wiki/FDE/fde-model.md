# FDE Model

## Summary
The FDE model is an embedded delivery model where engineers and strategists work inside the customer's real workflow and are accountable for business outcomes, not only tickets or man-months. For Consilium, this is a strategic lens for Japan IT transformation, AI-SDLC rollout, and AI-native service design.

## Key Points
- FDE should be treated as a business model, not only a job title.
- The core shift is from man-month delivery to outcome-based activation.
- Palantir is the originator pattern: embedded teams, ontology, bootcamp, platform leverage, and AI FDE automation.
- OpenAI and Anthropic adopting FDE-like enterprise deployment is a signal that AI vendors need embedded activation to move customers from POC to production.
- The durable layer is service-as-software: repeated workflows, reusable ontology, automation, and measurable outcomes.

## Why It Matters
Japan enterprise AI adoption is constrained by risk aversion, procurement structure, language/culture fit, and the weakness of POC-only delivery. FDE is a possible answer because it embeds technical capability closer to the business owner and makes outcomes explicit.

## Details
Core concepts mapped from the Opus Home FDE tab:

- Service-as-Software: services delivered with software-like repeatability, measurement, and compounding reuse.
- Delta + Echo pair: Delta handles technical implementation; Echo handles domain, business, and deployment strategy.
- Outcome-based contract: pricing and renewal tie to a measurable business KPI.
- Ontology: reusable model of the customer's operating reality, including objects, relationships, permissions, and workflows.
- Bootcamp: short, high-intensity proof using real customer data before a longer engagement.
- AI FDE: automation layer that lets one human FDE team control more workflow surface through agents and platform actions.
