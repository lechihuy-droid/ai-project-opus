# FDE Roadmap

## Summary
This roadmap turns the FDE thesis into staged capability building. The current framing has three phases: validate, build capability, then productize methodology and AI FDE automation.

## Key Points
- Phase 1 validates the model with a narrow pilot and explicit KPI.
- Phase 2 builds internal capability: FDE-grade people, platform tooling, Japan-specific bootcamp, case studies, and partnerships.
- Phase 3 productizes the methodology and uses AI FDE automation to increase leverage.
- The roadmap is only useful if each phase produces artifacts: pilot template, ontology starter, bootcamp script, case study, dashboard, or agent workflow.

## Why It Matters
FDE is easy to admire and hard to operationalize. A roadmap prevents the wiki from becoming only a strategy note.

## Details
Phase 1 - POC:
- Pick one or two customers or internal workflows with a clear AI use case.
- Pitch a narrow outcome-based pilot with a measurable KPI.
- Build a minimum domain ontology.
- Define risk-sharing and acceptance criteria.
- Document the FDE playbook.

Phase 2 - Capability:
- Train 5-10 FDE-grade engineers or simulate the profile through smaller internal projects.
- Build internal tooling for ontology, deployment automation, and evaluation.
- Create a Japan-specific bootcamp methodology.
- Produce case studies in Japanese.
- Build partner channels.

Phase 3 - Scale:
- Add an AI FDE layer on top of the methodology.
- Productize bootcamp and ontology by vertical.
- Move a meaningful share of revenue to outcome contracts.
- Expand from Japan credentials into APAC.

## Application To OPUS ANIMUS
For Huy, the first internal test should not be a full customer pilot. It should be a small Opus workflow where an embedded operator role, ontology, metric, and review loop are all explicit.
