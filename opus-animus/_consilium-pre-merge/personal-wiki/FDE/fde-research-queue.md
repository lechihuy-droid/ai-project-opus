# FDE Research Queue

## Summary
This page stores the durable reading queue for the FDE topic. The dashboard can show the queue, but the wiki should decide why each source matters and what it may change.

## Key Points
- Primary sources should define the baseline: Palantir FDE, Palantir AI FDE docs, and direct enterprise deployment artifacts.
- Market signals should show whether the model is spreading: hiring/adoption evidence, enterprise partnerships, and customer cases.
- Expert analysis is useful for explanation and positioning, but should not outweigh primary evidence.
- Each source should eventually answer one question: does it change strategy, re-skill, investment thinking, or an Opus workflow?

## Why It Matters
Without a research queue, FDE news becomes a feed. The queue turns sources into a review pipeline.

## Details
Current priority sources from Opus Home:

- A Day in the Life of a Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer.
- Palantir AI FDE official docs.
- Forward Deployed Engineer - The Hottest Job in AI.
- Marty Cagan - The FDE Concept.
- Everest Group - Palantir as Category of One.

Review method:

1. Identify whether the source is primary, market signal, or commentary.
2. Map it to one FDE page: model, Japan gap, roadmap, adoption radar, or investment thesis.
3. Decide update label: keep, change, test, ignore, or research.
4. Add only repeated or decision-changing evidence to evergreen pages.

## Application To OPUS ANIMUS
Use this page to drive a weekly FDE review. ChatGPT mobile can ask which queued source should be read next; desktop Codex should update pages after deeper inspection.
