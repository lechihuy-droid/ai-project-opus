# HoReN: Normalized Hopfield Retrieval for Large-Scale Sequential Model Editing

## Summary
arXiv:2605.08143v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language models encode vast factual knowledge that inevitably becomes outdated or incorrect after deployment, yet retraining is costly prohibitive, motivating model editing in lifelong settings that updates targeted behavior without harming the rest of the model.
