# Automatic Causal Fairness Analysis with LLM-Generated Reporting

## Summary
arXiv:2604.27011v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: AutoML, intended as the process of automating the application of machine learning to real-world problems, is a key step for AI popularisation. Most AutoML frameworks are not accounting for the potential lack of fairness in the training data and in the corresponding predictions. We introduce \textsc{FairMind}, a software prototype aiming to automatise fairness analysis at the dataset level. We achieve that by resorting to the assumptions of the \emph{standard fairness model}, recently proposed by Ple\v{c}ko and Bareinboim.
