# Zig Project's Anti-AI Contribution Policy

## Summary
The Zig project has a strict anti-AI contribution policy, prohibiting the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) for issues, pull requests, and comments on the bug tracker.

## Key Points
- No LLMs for issues.
- No LLMs for pull requests.
- No LLMs for comments on the bug tracker, including translation.

## Why It Matters
This policy matters because it reflects the project's values and commitment to human-driven development, ensuring that contributions are made by individuals rather than AI systems.

## Details
The policy is outlined on the Zig website and applies to all aspects of the project, including issues, pull requests, and comments on the bug tracker. English is encouraged as the primary language, but contributors are welcome to post in their native language and rely on others to use their own translation tools.

## Application To OPUS ANIMUS
The Zig project's anti-AI contribution policy can serve as a model for other open-source projects, highlighting the importance of human involvement and oversight in software development. This approach can help maintain the integrity and quality of contributions, ensuring that they align with the project's goals and values.
