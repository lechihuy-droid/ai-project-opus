# Human-in-the-Loop Systems for Agentic Workflows

## Summary
A decoupled Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) system architecture for controlled autonomy in agentic workflows, treating human oversight as an independent system component.

## Key Points
- Decoupled HITL system architecture
- Human oversight as an independent system component
- Improved transparency, accountability, and trustworthiness

## Why It Matters
This concept matters because it enables the development of more reliable and trustworthy AI systems, which is crucial for their deployment in real-world applications, particularly in areas that require human oversight and accountability.

## Details
The proposed system separates human interaction management from application workflows, allowing for greater flexibility and scalability. This architecture can be applied to various domains, including healthcare, finance, and transportation, where human oversight is essential.

## Application To OPUS ANIMUS
The implementation of this HITL system can enhance the autonomy and decision-making capabilities of OPUS ANIMUS, enabling it to operate more effectively in complex environments and make more informed decisions.
