## Summary
The OpenAI Microsoft AGI clause has been deceased. The clause stated that if AGI was achieved, Microsoft's commercial IP rights to OpenAI's technology would be null and void.

## Key points
- The clause has been part of the Microsoft and OpenAI relationship for many years
- The clause has been deceased as of April 27, 2026
- The clause stated that Microsoft's commercial IP rights to OpenAI's technology would be null and void if AGI was achieved

## Details
The clause was first introduced as part of the partnership between Microsoft and OpenAI. The clause was seen as a way to ensure that if AGI was achieved, the technology would be available for the greater good, rather than being controlled by a single company.
