# Formal Science: Scalable Human-in-the-Loop Autoformalisation of Science

## Summary
Formalising informal mathematical reasoning into formally verifiable code is a significant challenge for large language models. FormalScience is a domain-agnostic human-in-the-loop agentic pipeline that enables a single domain expert to produce syntactically correct and semantically aligned formal proofs of informal reasoning.

## Key Points
- FormalScience aids autoformalisation in scientific domains.
- It is a human-in-the-loop agentic pipeline.
- It enables domain experts to produce formally verifiable code without deep formal language experience.

## Why It Matters
FormalScience matters because it can significantly reduce the cost and increase the efficiency of formalising informal mathematical reasoning. This can lead to breakthroughs in various scientific fields, such as physics, by providing a scalable and reliable method for verifying the correctness of mathematical proofs.

## Details
FormalScience applies to physics, constructing FormalPhysics, a database of formal proofs of informal reasoning in physics. The pipeline consists of a single domain expert working with a large language model to produce formally verifiable code. This approach has the potential to tackle formalisation challenges in scientific domains that modern LLMs and agentic approaches have yet to address.

## Application To OPUS ANIMUS
FormalScience can be applied to OPUS ANIMUS by integrating it into the existing pipeline for autoformalisation of scientific knowledge. This can enhance the reliability and efficiency of the pipeline, enabling the system to tackle more complex formalisation challenges and provide more accurate results.
