# Current Beliefs

## Summary
This page is the short list of beliefs Codex should use when turning the wiki into decisions. It is not a news feed. It is the working mental model for AI trends, re-skill direction, and investment thinking.

## Key Points
- AI agents and coding agents are becoming an operating layer, not just developer tools.
- The highest-value re-skill direction is learning to design, supervise, evaluate, and productize AI workflows.
- Investment thinking should focus on durable second-order effects: compute demand, workflow displacement, enterprise adoption, AI infra, and Japan/Asia supply-chain exposure.
- Most raw seed pages are weak evidence until they repeat across sources or change a decision.
- 2026-05-20 update: confidence increased that the durable layer is operational control around agents: safe execution, audit trails, memory/context, evaluation, and workflow-specific deployment.

## Why It Matters
Without explicit beliefs, the wiki becomes a searchable archive. With explicit beliefs, Codex can compare new sources against the current model and say what should change.
