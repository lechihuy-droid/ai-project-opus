Abstract: Inferring the identity of wildlife species from daily movement data alone is a challenging task. We train sequence models on large-scale, 7-species GPS trajectories from the Movebank platform. Trajectories models are evaluated using a protocol in which entire telemetry studies or regions are heldout during testing. We compare Transformer-based sequence models to LSTM, CNN, and Temporal Convolutional Networks, and find that Transformers consistently achieve higher balanced accuracy with gains of approximately 8 to 22 percentage points, depending on the species and experimental setting.
