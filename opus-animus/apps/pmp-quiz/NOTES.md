## 📋 Quá Trình & Kiến Thức PMP

### 5 Process Groups (PMBOK)

1. **Initiating** (Khởi Tạo)
   - Phát triển Project Charter
   - Identify Stakeholders

2. **Planning** (Lập Kế Hoạch)
   - Scope Planning, Schedule, Cost
   - Risk Planning, Quality Planning

3. **Executing** (Thực Hiện)
   - Thực hiện công việc
   - Acquire & Develop Team
   - Manage Communications

4. **Monitoring & Controlling** (Giám Sát)
   - Monitor Work Performance
   - Control Schedule, Cost, Quality

5. **Closing** (Đóng)
   - Close Project/Phase
   - Archive Lessons Learned

### 10 Knowledge Areas (PMBOK)

1. Integration Management
2. Scope Management
3. Schedule Management
4. Cost Management
5. Quality Management
6. Resource Management
7. Communications Management
8. Risk Management
9. Procurement Management
10. Stakeholder Management

## 🎓 PMP Exam Information

### Exam Details

- Duration: 3 hours 50 minutes
- Questions: 180 multiple-choice
- Passing Score: ~106/180 questions correct (58%)
- Cost: ~$555 USD (for members)
- Validity: 3 years
- Renewal: Through PDU (Professional Development Units)

### Requirements

- 36 months PM experience (3-5 years)
- 35 hours PM training
- High school diploma/equivalent

### Format in Real Exam

- All multiple choice
- 4 options per question
- Scenario-based questions
- Situational questions
