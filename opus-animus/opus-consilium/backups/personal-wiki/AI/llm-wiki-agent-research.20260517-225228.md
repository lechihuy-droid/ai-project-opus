## Summary
The OPUS ANIMUS project involves integrating AI explanations and guidance into an app, focusing on PMP exams and Vietnamese language support. This page discusses the capture of meaningful prompts reflecting thinking patterns, problem framing, and AI usage.

## Key Points
- The project uses various files and APIs, including the gemini-1.5-flash model.
- The user exceeded their current quota, with errors pointing to https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits for more information.
