# FDE Adoption Radar

## Summary
This page tracks who is adopting FDE-like enterprise AI deployment patterns. It turns the dashboard's actor map and daily news tags into a durable wiki frame.

## Key Points
- Palantir is the originator pattern and benchmark.
- OpenAI and Anthropic are adopting FDE-like enterprise deployment as frontier models need activation inside customer workflows.
- AWS, Google, and Microsoft have partial equivalents through solution architects, customer engineers, professional services, and cloud partner motions.
- Japan SIers are the opportunity zone: they have customer access but may lack outcome accountability, ontology reuse, and AI-native deployment loops.
- Daily news should be interpreted as evidence only when it changes the adoption radar, not as standalone noise.

## Why It Matters
FDE is investable and personally actionable only if adoption spreads beyond Palantir. The radar prevents overreacting to single articles and focuses on repeated signals.

## Details
Actor groups currently tracked in Opus Home:

- Palantir
- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- Microsoft
- Google
- Consulting + AI
- SIer Japan
- FDE Model

Signal tiers:

- Tier 1: AI lab + consulting/SIer partnership, three or more actor groups, or FDE pattern plus partnership signal.
- Tier 2: two actor groups or strong FDE model keyword.
- Tier 3: single actor mention.

Use Tier 1 as update-worthy evidence. Use Tier 2 as watchlist evidence. Treat Tier 3 as background unless it repeats.
