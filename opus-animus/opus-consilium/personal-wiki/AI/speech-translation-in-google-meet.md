## Summary
Speech translation in Google Meet is now rolling out to mobile devices. This feature allows for real-time translation of spoken language, enabling more effective communication across language barriers.

## Key points
- Speech translation in Google Meet is now available on mobile devices
- The feature enables real-time translation of spoken language
- It aims to improve communication across language barriers

## Details
The rollout of speech translation in Google Meet to mobile devices is a significant update, as it expands the reach of this feature to a wider range of users. This technology has the potential to greatly enhance collaboration and communication in multilingual environments.
