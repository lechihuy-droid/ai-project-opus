## Summary
The Karpathy LLM Wiki pattern is a method for creating a personal wiki using a large language model (LLM) as a research librarian. The LLM reads and processes input documents, creating and updating wiki pages, and linking them together. This approach allows for efficient knowledge management and querying.

## Key points
- LLM reads input documents and creates wiki pages
- Wiki pages are linked together for efficient querying
- The approach is suitable for personal or team knowledge management

## Details
The Karpathy LLM Wiki pattern consists of three main components: raw input documents, wiki pages, and a schema file. The LLM reads the raw documents, extracts key information, and creates or updates wiki pages. The schema file defines the structure and organization of the wiki.
