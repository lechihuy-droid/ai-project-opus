# Learning in the Fisher Subspace: A Guided Initialization for LoRA Fine-Tuning

## Summary
arXiv:2605.01046v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: LoRA adapts large language models (LLMs) by restricting updates to low-rank subspaces of pre-trained weights. While this substantially reduces training cost, the effectiveness of adaptation critically depends on which subspace is chosen at initialization: a poor initialization that allocates capacity to task-irrelevant directions can severely hinder downstream performance. Existing initialization strategies primarily rely on the intrinsic properties of pre-trained weights, implicitly assuming that weight geometry alone reflects task relevance. However, such criteria overlook how the model interacts with the downstream data distribution.
