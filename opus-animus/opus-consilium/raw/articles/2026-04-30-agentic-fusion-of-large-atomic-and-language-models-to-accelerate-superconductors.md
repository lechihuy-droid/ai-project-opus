# Agentic Fusion of Large Atomic and Language Models to Accelerate Superconductors Discovery

**Source:** hf-papers
**URL:** https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.23758
**Published:** 2026-04-30 20:30 UTC
**Topic:** AI

The discovery of novel materials is critical for global energy and quantum technology transitions. While deep learning has fundamentally reshaped this landscape, existing predictive or generative models typically operate in isolation, lacking the autonomous orchestration required to execute the full discovery process. Here we present ElementsClaw, an agentic framework for materials discovery that synergizes Large Atomic Models (LAMs) with Large Language Models (LLMs). In response to varied human queries, ElementsClaw orchestrates a suite of LAM tools finetuned from our proposed 1-billion-parameter model Elements for atomic-scale numerical computation, while leveraging LLMs for high-level semantic reasoning.
