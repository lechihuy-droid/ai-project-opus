Abstract: Recursive reasoning systems alternate between acquiring new evidence and refining an accumulated understanding. Two design choices are typically left implicit: how to represent the evolving reasoning state, and when to stop iterating. This paper addresses both. We represent the reasoning state as an epistemic state graph encoding extracted claims, evidential relations, open questions, and confidence weights. We define the order-gap as the distance between the states reached by expand-then-consolidate versus consolidate-then-expand; a small order-gap suggests that the two orderings agree and further iteration is unlikely to help.
