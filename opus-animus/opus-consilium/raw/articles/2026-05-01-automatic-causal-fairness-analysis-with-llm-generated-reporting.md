# Automatic Causal Fairness Analysis with LLM-Generated Reporting

**Source:** arxiv-lg
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27011
**Published:** 2026-05-01 04:00 UTC
**Topic:** AI

arXiv:2604.27011v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: AutoML, intended as the process of automating the application of machine learning to real-world problems, is a key step for AI popularisation. Most AutoML frameworks are not accounting for the potential lack of fairness in the training data and in the corresponding predictions. We introduce \textsc{FairMind}, a software prototype aiming to automatise fairness analysis at the dataset level. We achieve that by resorting to the assumptions of the \emph{standard fairness model}, recently proposed by Ple\v{c}ko and Bareinboim. This allows for a sound fairness evaluation in terms of causal effects, based on \emph{counterfactual} queries involving the target, possibly confounders and mediators, and the different values of an input feature we regard
