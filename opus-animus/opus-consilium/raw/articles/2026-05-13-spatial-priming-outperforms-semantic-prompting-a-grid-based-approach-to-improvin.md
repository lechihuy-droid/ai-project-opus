Abstract: The automated extraction of data from scientific charts is a critical task for large-scale literature analysis. While multimodal Large Language Models (LLMs) show promise, their accuracy on non-standardized charts remains a challenge. This raises a key research question: what is the most effective strategy to improve model performance (high-level semantic priming) or low-level spatial priming? This paper presents a comparative investigation into these two distinct strategies. We describe our exploratory experiments with semantic methods, such as a two-stage metadata-first framework and Chain-of-Thought, which failed to produce a statistically significant improvement.
