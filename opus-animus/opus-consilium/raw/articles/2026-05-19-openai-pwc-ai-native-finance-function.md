OpenAI and PwC are partnering to build the first AI-native finance function, embedding agentic AI into corporate finance workflows under human supervision. The collaboration reimagines the office of the CFO by integrating AI agents into planning, forecasting, reporting, procurement, payments, treasury, tax, and accounting close. OpenAI's finance organization serves as "customer zero" piloting AI agents before scaling to enterprise clients. Using Codex, Workspace Agents, and Model Context Protocols, finance teams build bespoke applications without traditional software development. Human-in-the-loop governance: finance professionals define guardrails and policies while AI agents handle routine tasks. Early adopters may reduce operational costs by 20-30% and improve forecast accuracy by up to 15%. PwC provides consulting expertise to integrate AI agents into existing ERP, treasury, and data systems with compliance and auditability.
