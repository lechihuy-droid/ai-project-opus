Abstract: Background & Objectives: In the last decade, Machine learning research has grown rapidly, but large models are reaching their soft limits demonstrating diminishing returns and still lack solid reasoning abilities. These limits could be surpassed through synergistic combination of Machine Learning scalability and rigid reasoning. Methods: In this work, we propose a theoretical framework for reasoning through object-relations in an automated manner integrated with Artificial Neural Networks. We present a formal analysis of the Reasoning, and we show the theory in practice through a paradigm integrating Reasoning and Machine Learning.
