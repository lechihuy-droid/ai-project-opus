Abstract: Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) has become the standard for fine-tuning large pre-trained models at reduced computational cost. However, its low-rank point-estimate updates limit expressiveness, leave a persistent gap relative to full fine-tuning accuracy, and provide no built-in uncertainty quantification, limiting its applicability in settings where reliability matters as much as accuracy. We introduce BaLoRA, a Bayesian extension of LoRA with a novel input-adaptive Bayesian parameterization of LoRA matrices that adds minimal parameters and compute.
