# Addressing Performance Saturation for LLM RL via Precise Entropy Curve Control

Abstract: Reinforcement learning (RL) has unlocked complex reasoning abilities in large language models (LLMs). However, most RL algorithms suffer from performance saturation, preventing further gains as RL training scales. This problem can be characterized by the collapse of entropy, a key diagnostic for exploration in RL. Existing attempts have tried to prevent entropy collapse through regularization or clipping, but their resulting entropy curves often exhibit instability in the long term, which hinders performance gains. In this paper, we introduce Entrocraft, a simple rejection-sampling approach that realizes any user-customized entropy schedule by biasing the advantage distributions.
