# Rethinking Layer Redundancy in Large Language Models: Calibration Objectives and Search for Depth Pruning

**Source:** arxiv-lg
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24938
**Published:** 2026-04-29 04:00 UTC
**Topic:** AI

arXiv:2604.24938v1 Announce Type: new 
Abstract: Depth pruning improves the inference efficiency of large language models by removing Transformer blocks. Prior work has focused on importance criteria and search algorithms, often treating layer redundancy as an inherent structural property of pretrained networks. In contrast, we adopt a \emph{functional perspective}, where redundancy is jointly influenced by the model and the evaluation objective, suggesting that a universal ranking may not be sufficient. Through an empirical study across three LLM families, two calibration objectives, and seven search algorithms, we observe that different objectives yield qualitatively different redundant layers, and that perplexity and downstream accuracy rankings do not consistently align.
