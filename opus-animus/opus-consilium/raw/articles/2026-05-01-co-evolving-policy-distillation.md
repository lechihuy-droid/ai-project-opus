Abstract: RLVR and OPD have become standard paradigms for post-training. We provide a unified analysis of these two paradigms in consolidating multiple expert capabilities into a single model, identifying capability loss in different ways: mixed RLVR suffers from inter-capability divergence cost, while the pipeline of first training experts and then performing OPD, though avoiding divergence, fails to fully absorb teacher capabilities due to large behavioral pattern gaps between teacher and student.
