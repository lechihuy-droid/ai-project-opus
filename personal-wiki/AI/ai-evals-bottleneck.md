# AI Evaluations as the New Compute Bottleneck

## Summary
AI evaluations are becoming a significant bottleneck in compute resources, impacting the development and deployment of AI models.

## Key Points
- AI evaluations require substantial computational power
- The cost of AI evaluations can be a significant bottleneck in the development process

## Why It Matters
Understanding the impact of AI evaluations on compute resources is crucial for optimizing AI development and deployment processes, which can significantly affect the efficiency and cost-effectiveness of AI projects.

## Details
The increasing complexity of AI models and the need for rigorous evaluation processes have led to a surge in computational requirements, making AI evaluations a new compute bottleneck. This bottleneck can hinder the development and deployment of AI models, ultimately affecting their performance and reliability.

## Application To OPUS ANIMUS
Optimizing AI evaluation processes can help reduce computational costs and improve the efficiency of AI development and deployment in OPUS ANIMUS, enabling more effective and efficient AI-powered solutions.
